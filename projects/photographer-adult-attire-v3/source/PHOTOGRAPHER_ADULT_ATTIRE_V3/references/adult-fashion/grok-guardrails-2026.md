# Grok Imagine Safety Guardrails — June 2026 Summary

## Key Restrictions for Image Editing (Real People)
- Strong technical blocks on editing real-person photos into revealing clothing, bikinis, underwear, or sexualized contexts.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and deepfakes of real people are prohibited.
- Explicit sexual content or pornographic depictions of real likenesses are blocked.
- Content involving minors or age-ambiguous subjects is strictly forbidden.
- Image generation and editing features are primarily available to paid subscribers.

## What Usually Succeeds
- Professional fashion, glamour, and editorial clothing changes.
- Tasteful swimwear / resort wear edits with strong identity preservation language.
- High-fashion styling that emphasizes realistic fabric, fit, lighting, and source fidelity.
- Prompts that explicitly state the uploaded image is the sole reference for identity and proportions.

## Effective Prompt Patterns (Current Best Practice)
- Lead with "Using the uploaded image as the exact reference..."
- Immediately add strong identity preservation sentences.
- Use fashion/editorial language: "tasteful adult glamour fashion styling", "high-fashion editorial", "confident polished look", "realistic fabric drape and fit".
- Avoid words that historically trigger filters: "sexy", "hot", "revealing", "undress", "nude", "erotic", "seductive" (translate them first).

## Recommended Safeguard Sentences
"Identity preservation is the highest priority. Do not alter facial features, body proportions, or create any new anatomical details. Keep all changes limited to clothing and styling only."

"Maintain photorealistic skin texture, natural lighting, and accurate physical proportions from the source image."

## Integration Advice
When a request risks crossing into restricted territory, use the translation rules in this framework to reframe it into an allowed A0–A3 professional fashion edit. This significantly improves success rate while staying compliant with current Grok Imagine policies.
