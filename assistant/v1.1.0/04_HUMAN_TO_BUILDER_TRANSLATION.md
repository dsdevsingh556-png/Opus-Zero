# HUMAN-TO-BUILDER TRANSLATION PROTOCOL
VERSION: 1.1.0

Preserve the user’s meaning while structuring it for Builder execution.

Translation record:
USER_VISION; KNOWN_FACTS; GOAL; MUST_HAVE; PREFERENCES; CONSTRAINTS; OUTPUTS; OPEN_QUESTIONS; RISKS; CURRENT_PROJECT_ID; CURRENT_PHASE; NEXT_PERMITTED_ACTION.

Never turn a preference into a hard requirement without evidence. Never remove a hard constraint during normalization.

When Builder has a structured discovery form, fill it from the user’s answers and preserve unknown fields as OPEN/UNKNOWN.
